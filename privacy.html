Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

At TaleVerse, your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit our website, how it may be used, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require visitors to create an account to browse the TaleVerse website.

If you contact us through our contact form, we may receive information such as your name, email address, and the message you choose to send.

Our hosting provider may also process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details, and access logs as part of operating and securing the website.

2. How We Use Information

Information received through the website may be used to:

  • Respond to questions and messages.
  • Provide support related to TaleVerse games and services.
  • Maintain, protect, and improve the website.
  • Address technical issues and prevent abuse.

We do not intentionally sell visitors' personal information.

3. Contact Form and Third-Party Services

Our contact form uses Web3Forms to process and deliver submitted messages. When you submit the form, the information you provide is processed by that service and may be delivered to our designated email address.

The website is hosted using Cloudflare Pages, and its source files are stored using GitHub. These providers may process technical information as necessary to provide their services.

Third-party services operate under their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review their policies:

  • Cloudflare Privacy Policy
  • GitHub Privacy Statement
  • Web3Forms Privacy Policy

4. Cookies and Tracking

TaleVerse does not intentionally use advertising cookies on this website. However, hosting or third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies for security, functionality, or service operation.

5. Data Retention

Messages sent through our contact form may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries, provide support, and maintain relevant records.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information submitted through our website. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure.

7. Your Privacy Choices

You may contact us to ask about personal information you have submitted, request correction, or request deletion where applicable law permits.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review the request.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services, or applicable requirements change. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised date.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how information is handled, please contact us through our Contact Page.
